Ubuntu Health for hospital groups

Deliver personalised care, unify all hospital processes and enhance financial performance.

Ubuntu Health for hospitals

is a cloud-based, AI-driven digital health platform purpose built to elevate patient care, optimise your Hospital Group’s operations and enhance financial performance. It achieves this by seamlessly integrating fragmented systems, unlocking AI-powered insights for data-driven decisions and streamlining administrative processes.

Hospital features

Patient Management & Engagement

Multi-Channel Patient Registration · Online Booking & Appointment Management · Telehealth Consultations & Secure Messaging · Personalised Patient Portal

In-Patient Management

AI-powered bed allocation · Streamlined medication ordering/dispensing/administration · Integration with patient monitoring devices · Automated discharge planning tools

Electronic Patient Medical Records

Digitally create, store, and manage patient medical records

Documents

Upload, store and retrieve patient and administrative documentation, on demand

Supply Chain

Order, track, receive and manage PPE, medication and essential inventory from suppliers

Inventory

AI demand forecasting for medications and medical consumables, optimising inventory levels and reducing waste.

Pharmacy

Prescribe, send orders, track medication levels and link to supply chain management for automatic restocking.

Healthcare Heartbeat

A single view of all operations of your facility (doctors on duty, patients being treated, backlogs) in real-time

Reporting & Analytics

Providing actionable insights that feed into key performance indicators (KPI’s), such as patient outcomes, operational efficiency and financial performance.

Finance

Optimise finances through a secure integration with your existing financial systems, ensuring accurate revenue capture, streamlined claims processing and reduced administrative costs. Real-time insights improve forecasting and resource allocation across the value chain.

Holistic hospital care at your fingertips.
