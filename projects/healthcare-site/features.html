Ubuntu Health for practices

Streamline quality patient care, from booking to billing.

Ubuntu Health for practices

is a cloud-based, AI-driven digital health platform curated to enhance patient care, simplify consults and streamline financial management.

Through intuitive Doctor and Patient mobile & web apps, Ubuntu Health simplifies practice management and empowers you to deliver best-of-breed care to your patients.

Doctor features

Booking management

Seamlessly manage availability, slots and bookings from existing and new patients through our AI booking engine.

Teleconsults

Secure in-app video consults, linked to billing, eprescriptions, ereports and digital consult notes.

AI medical transcription

Ubuntu’s AI listens, transcribes and summarises consults, allowing doctors to focus solely on patient.

Medical aid claim submissions

Ubuntu’s integration with Medical Aids allows for seamless submission and tracking of claims.

EHR

Securely store and access comprehensive patient health data, including medical history, diagnoses, lab results, and prescriptions, on-demand.

e-Prescriptions

Doctors can securely issue, refill, or cancel prescriptions directly from the app, ensuring accuracy and compliance with healthcare regulations.

Secure in-app chat

End-to-end encrypted chat feature between Doctor and patient, ensuring that personal health information remains confidential. Doctors can manage patient questions & followups all within a protected, POPIA-compliant environment.

Patient features

Directory of all medical professionals / facilities

Find trusted, Google rated healthcare providers with ease using Ubuntu’s Directory

Medication tracker

Set personalised reminders for medications, vitamins and supplements, ensuring you never miss a dose.

Live health news

Ubuntu’s live feed of verified health news ensures that patients are never left in the dark.

Digital bookings

Easily book consults with doctors through the web, mobile or WhatsApp channels.

Medical documentation library

Access a secure repository of all your medical consults, diagnoses, lab results and prescriptions, across doctors, from the comfort of your Ubuntu app.

Secure in-app chat

End-to-end encrypted chat feature between provider and patient, ensuring that personal health information remains confidential. Share symptoms, ask questions, or follow up on treatments in real time, all within a protected, POPIA-compliant environment.
